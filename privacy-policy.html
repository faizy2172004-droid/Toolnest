Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how ToolNest handles information when you use this website.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy applies to ToolNest and the free online tools available on this website. It describes the information that may be processed when you visit or use the site.

2. Information you enter into tools

Many ToolNest tools are designed to process text, images, calculations, or files directly in your browser. For these tools, the information you enter or files you select are processed on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to ToolNest servers by the tool itself. Your browser, extensions, or third-party scripts may operate separately. Do not use a browser-based tool as a secure vault for confidential information.

Some features may rely on third-party libraries or services. If a feature is changed to send information to an external service, this policy should be updated before that feature is introduced.

3. Hosting and technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. This can include IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and server or security logs. We do not use this information to identify you personally unless needed to investigate abuse, protect the service, or comply with law.

4. Cookies, advertising and analytics

ToolNest may display advertising in the future, including through Google AdSense, if the site is accepted into an advertising programme. Advertising providers may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads, subject to their own policies and your available choices. Advertising is not guaranteed to be active on every visit.

If analytics, advertising, or consent-management services are enabled, this policy must be reviewed and updated to accurately name the providers and describe their data practices. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some site features may not work as intended if cookies are blocked.

5. Links to other websites

ToolNest may link to third-party websites or use third-party resources. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review the relevant provider's privacy policy before using its services.

6. Children's privacy

ToolNest is a general-purpose website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit information through email and can control cookies using your browser settings. To ask a privacy question or make a request concerning information you have sent to us, email fuls2172004@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, tools, or services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after an update means you have seen the revised policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, visit our Contact page or email fuls2172004@gmail.com.
