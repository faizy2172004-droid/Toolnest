IMAGE TOOL

WebP Converter

Convert JPG, PNG and other supported images to lightweight WebP files in your browser.

Your converted image preview will appear here.

Conversion uses the browser canvas. The selected image is not uploaded to ToolNest. Some image formats, such as animated GIF, may convert only their first frame.

How it works

This tool runs in your browser using built-in web technologies. Your input is processed on your device and is not sent to a ToolNest processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? Yes. There is no paid API or subscription required.

Do I need an account? No account is needed to use this tool.
