DATE TOOL

Date Duration Calculator

Calculate the exact number of days, weeks, months and years between two dates.

Choose two dates to calculate the duration.

The day count uses calendar dates in your local timezone and excludes the start date. Inclusive day count is also shown.

How it works

This tool runs in your browser using built-in web technologies. Your input is processed on your device and is not sent to a ToolNest processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? Yes. There is no paid API or subscription required.

Do I need an account? No account is needed to use this tool.
