About this word counter

Use this free word counter to check the length of essays, articles, emails, reports, social posts and other writing. Results update as you type, so you do not need to submit your text or create an account.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste or type your text in the writing area.
  2. Review the live word, character, sentence and paragraph counts.
  3. Use the copy button to copy your text or clear the box to start again.

Frequently asked questions

How does the word counter count words?

It counts groups of text separated by whitespace. This gives a practical estimate for most everyday writing.

Does it count spaces as characters?

Yes. The character total includes spaces and line breaks. A separate figure shows characters without whitespace.

Is my text uploaded?

The counter runs in your browser. The text you enter is not intentionally sent to a ToolNest server.
